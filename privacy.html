Privacy Policy for TableQuest

Last updated: [DATE]

TableQuest ("the app") is a character sheet and combat initiative tracker for tabletop role-playing games. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: TableQuest does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, because it has no server and no way to send information anywhere.

Information We Do Not Collect

TableQuest does not collect or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (there are no user accounts)
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, or any other device permissions — the app does not request any
  • Payment or financial information

How Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter into TableQuest — characters (including any portrait you add), combatants, saved parties, saved encounters, and your enemy/NPC library — is stored using your browser's local storage, entirely on your own device. This data:

  • Is never transmitted to TableQuest's developer, to any server, or to any third party
  • Is not accessible to anyone but you
  • Stays on your device until you clear it yourself, using the app's own "Reset" and "Backup & Restore" tools, or by clearing your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app

The app includes an optional Export/Import feature that lets you save your data to a file of your own choosing (for example, to move it between your own devices). This file is created and saved directly by your browser to a location you choose — it is never uploaded anywhere by the app itself.

Third-Party Services

TableQuest does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or any other third-party tracking service. It makes no network requests while running, other than loading its own code, styling, and icon assets when the app is first opened or updated.

Data Sharing

Because TableQuest does not collect any data, there is none to share, sell, or disclose to any third party, for advertising purposes or otherwise.

Children's Privacy

TableQuest does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no data from any user, this applies equally regardless of age.

Your Choices

Since your data is stored only on your own device and never reaches us, there is nothing on our end to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You are always in full control of it directly within the app, or by clearing your browser's storage for this site.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of TableQuest adds a feature that does collect or transmit data — this page will be updated to reflect that, along with a new "Last updated" date above.
